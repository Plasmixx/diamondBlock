Why diamondBlock

Zero paywalls or Pro tiers

The leading app blocker charges up to $99 a year. With diamondBlock, every focus schedule, strict block, and deep-work mode is unlocked for everyone from day one.

100% open source and local-first

Strict focus modes

Built for families, not for spying

Parents set the limits

A parent picks which apps are blocked and when, using Apple's Family Controls on their child's iPhone or iPad. No separate tracking app, and no account needed for the child to be protected.

Kids earn their way back

Instead of a hard lock with no way out, a blocked app can be earned back by solving problems at the child's level, waiting out a timer, or tapping phones with a friend.

For yourself, too

Adults can use the same blocks for their own focus. diamondBlock is never used to monitor employees or other adults — it is for families and for your own screen time.

How it works

1. Block it

Pick the apps to block, or block everything except a few you allow.

2. Get stopped

Open a blocked app and diamondBlock steps in instead of the feed.

3. Earn your way back

Solve problems at your level, wait out the timer, or tap phones with a friend.

4. Emergency keys

You start with 3 emergency exit keys, just in case something happens. They're never for sale.

Coming soon: diamondBlock for Schools

Teachers make the questions. Students earn their phone time by getting them right — 5 in a row, or 12 correct in total. The teacher picks the unlock time and whether hints are on, and the tools only work during school hours.

Calling and Messages stay unblockable, always. Emergency keys still exist, and unlocked time comes with warnings and a "one last message" button so nobody gets locked mid-sentence.

Questions, answered

Is it really free?

Yes. Every feature is unlocked for everyone, forever. There are no Pro tiers, and donations never unlock anything.

Does it track me or read my messages?

No. We never see what is inside your apps, your messages, your photos, or your browsing history, and we never track your location.

What is Family Controls?

It is Apple's system that lets a screen-time app block apps and set limits. That permission stays on your device — Apple does not send us your app usage, and neither do we collect it.

What if I need my phone in an emergency?

You start with 3 emergency exit keys, and they are never for sale. Calling and Messages are always unblockable.

Which phones will it work on?

iOS first, then Android. The waitlist will get an email as soon as the first version is ready.

How do I unlock a blocked app?

Solve problems at your level, wait out the timer, or tap phones with a friend. A teacher-set version for schools is coming too — see below.
