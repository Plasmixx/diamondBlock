1 Who we are

diamondBlock is a free, open-source, non-profit app blocker. It helps people set limits on their own phones, and it helps parents set limits on a child's iPhone or iPad using Apple's Family Controls.

diamondBlock is the “we” in these terms. Our website is diamondblock.org, and our app code is public under the MIT license at github.com/Plasmixx/diamondBlock.

These terms cover the website, the waitlist, and the app when it launches. Our Privacy Policy explains what information we collect and what we never collect — read it alongside these terms.

2 Free forever

Every diamondBlock feature is free for everyone, forever. There are no Pro tiers, no subscriptions, and no paywalls.

Donations never unlock anything. A person who donates gets exactly the same app as a person who doesn't — donations are optional support for the project, not a way to buy features.

3 The app hasn't launched yet

Right now, only the website and the waitlist are live. Joining the waitlist means we will email you once when the first version is ready — it does not give you access to an app that doesn't exist yet, and it does not promise a launch date.

Features described on the site — including the Schools version — are planned, and may change before launch. We will update this page if a planned feature changes in an important way.

4 Accounts

When accounts launch, your account will save your settings — like your math level — so unlock problems match you. You are responsible for keeping your account details safe and for anything done with your account.

A child does not need their own diamondBlock account to be protected by a parent's limits. Parents set limits on a child's device using Apple's Family Controls.

You can ask us to delete your account at any time by emailing help@diamondblock.org. When we say we will delete it, we delete it from our database — it does not stay in storage. See section 14.

5 Age & children

diamondBlock is designed for families, and children use it through a parent's limits — not through their own account.

  • Waitlist and accounts ask your age first. When you join the waitlist or create an account, we ask you to confirm you are 13 or older, or you are a parent/guardian signing up. We do not knowingly collect an email address or account details from a child under 13 without a parent.
  • A child does not need their own account. A parent sets limits on a child's device using Apple's Family Controls. No separate account is created for the child, and we do not collect a child's messages, photos, location, browsing history, or activity inside apps.
  • Schools: students in the Schools version register with a school ID through their school, not by giving us an email address on their own. Schools must have the proper permission to share student information with us before students use it — see section 9.

If you are a parent and you believe your child gave us an email address or account information without your involvement, email help@diamondblock.org and we will delete it.

6 Family Controls & blocking

diamondBlock uses Apple's Family Controls system to block apps and set time limits. That permission stays on your device, and you can remove it at any time in iOS Settings, which stops diamondBlock from blocking anything.

Blocking is a tool to help with focus — it is not a guarantee that a particular app will be blocked at a particular time. Apple controls the underlying Family Controls system, and iOS updates or settings changes can affect how blocking works.

diamondBlock is built for families and for your own screen time. It is never for monitoring employees or other adults.

7 Emergency keys

You start with 3 emergency exit keys, and they are never for sale. They are a safety valve for a genuine emergency — not a way around a limit you set for yourself.

Calling and Messages are always unblockable in diamondBlock, so a call to a parent or to emergency services is never behind a problem set.

Emergency keys do not guarantee access in every situation. If the phone itself, iOS, or Apple's Family Controls prevents the app from opening, an emergency key cannot override that.

8 Acceptable use

Use diamondBlock lawfully and fairly:

  • Don't use it to control, monitor, or restrict another adult without their agreement.
  • Don't try to break, abuse, or overload the website, waitlist, or app, or sign other people up without their permission.
  • For a child, limits must be set by a parent or guardian — not by a teacher or anyone else acting alone. Teacher tools in the Schools version work only during school hours, as described in section 9.

If you use diamondBlock in a way that harms others or breaks the law, we may suspend or close your account or waitlist entry.

9 diamondBlock for Schools

diamondBlock for Schools is a planned version of the app, not yet launched. When it launches:

  • Students register with a school ID, and the app knows their math and English level so questions match them.
  • Teachers create questions and choose the unlock rules (5 correct in a row, or 12 correct in total), whether hints are on, and how long an unlock lasts.
  • Teacher tools only work during the school hours set by the school's administrator when the school registers. Outside those hours, the tools are unavailable.
  • Calling and Messages stay unblockable for students, always, and emergency exit keys still exist.
  • Unlocked time comes with warnings as time runs out, and a “one last message” button to finish a thought — one use per unlock.

Schools with questions can reach us at outreach@diamondblock.org.

10 Donations & payments

Donations are voluntary — and not refundable

A donation is optional support for a free, non-profit project, not a purchase of a product or a feature. Because of that, donations are not refundable. Please only donate an amount you are comfortable giving.

Online donations aren't set up yet. When a donation service is enabled, payments will be handled by that service — for example Buy Me a Coffee or GitHub Sponsors, which pay out through Stripe — under that service's own terms and privacy policy. diamondBlock will not receive or store your full card number. We will update this section and the Privacy Policy to name the service when it goes live.

There are no subscriptions, no free trial, and no auto-renewal. A donation is a one-off gift unless you choose a recurring donation on the payment service's own page — and if you do, that service, not diamondBlock, handles the renewal, and its renewal terms are shown next to its own pay button. There is nothing in diamondBlock that renews, charges you again, or needs a reminder, because there is nothing to renew. You can stop a recurring donation at any time through the payment service.

If a donation link is ever wrong or a payment is taken in error, email help@diamondblock.org and we will look into it and help you contact the payment service.

11 AI, automation & how it was built

The app does not use AI on your data. diamondBlock does not have an AI chatbot, it does not send your information to an AI service, and it does not make automated decisions about you. Unlock problems are generated by ordinary code at the level you chose — for example, your math level — not by an AI model, and no AI system decides whether an app stays blocked.

How it was built: parts of diamondBlock, including some code and this website, were written with the help of AI coding tools. That is how the app was built, not what the app does when you use it. It does not change anything in this section: when you use diamondBlock, your data is not sent to an AI service.

Our logo image on the website was made with AI. It is a picture, not a person, and it is not presented as a real photo or a real person.

Safety: because there is no AI chatbot in diamondBlock, there is no chatbot to give a self-harm response. If you or someone near you is in crisis, do not rely on an app — call or message for help directly. Calling and Messages are always unblockable in diamondBlock, so you can always reach someone.
